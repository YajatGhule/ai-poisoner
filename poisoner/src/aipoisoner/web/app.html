How sure is this, and what can it not do?

Images: a ViT classifier (AUC about 0.87 on our 36-image test set). AI at 85% or more, Human below 60%, Unsure in between. Real photos with heavy edits or unusual lighting can land in Unsure. Generator metadata (C2PA, IPTC, Stable Diffusion settings) is trusted over the model when present.

Voices: a wav2vec2-based model scores every 4-second window. Reliable on Kokoro-style TTS voices, weaker on engines it never saw, which mostly come out Unsure. Spliced AI stretches are shown with timestamps.

Music: SONICS SpecTTTra scores up to 12 five-second windows. Suno is caught well, short Udio clips are often missed. The voice model is made for speech, so ignore its result for songs.

Listen live

SENTINEL listens through the microphone and checks every 4 seconds for an AI voice and every 5 seconds for AI music, as it goes. Play a voice note, a video or a song near the laptop, or speak. Nothing is recorded to disk or sent anywhere; when you stop, the whole recording is checked once more and you can play it back.
