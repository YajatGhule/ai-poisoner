What do these numbers mean, and what is not proven?

The search looks for the smallest change that moves your picture furthest inside the encoder that Stable Diffusion-type models use to read images. "Latent shift" is how far it moved, and it is compared with random noise of the same size.

This is measured on the encoder only. It has not been tested by training or running a full image generator on protected pictures, and published work shows that this kind of protection can be weakened by heavy compression, blurring or purpose-built removal tools. Treat it as making misuse harder, not impossible.
